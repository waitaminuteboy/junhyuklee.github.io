Other interests and activities

Internships

Job shadowing and internships that showed me how engineering works outside the classroom, from rehabilitation technology in Australia to a robotics workshop in Korea.

VALD Performance

Intern · Product development team · Summer after Grade 9 · Australia

VALD builds measurement technology that clinicians and coaches use to assess how people move. I job-shadowed its product development team for six weeks, seven hours a day, five days a week.

Alongside the engineers, I helped research and test rehabilitation devices. I also translated a product manual into Korean and served as the translator in a meeting with clients from South Korea.

Seeing how sensing technology could measure movement beyond what observation alone can show is what first pointed me toward my force plate project. Read about the force plate project →

  • 6 weeks with the product development team
  • Researched and tested rehabilitation devices
  • Translated a product manual into Korean
  • Translator for a meeting with clients from South Korea

Holiday Robotics

Intern · Product testing team · Spring 2025 – Summer 2026 · Korea

Holiday Robotics builds robotic systems that bring together mechanical structures, actuators, sensors such as load cells, electronic circuits and control software. I joined its engineering team as an intern in the spring of 2025, working with the product testing team.

I worked with the engineers about once a week during the school term and two to three times a week during vacations, through the summer of 2026. I checked incoming parts against their specifications, prepared assembly stations and helped assemble components. When a specification or an assembly step was unclear, I asked before going ahead.

Later, Holiday Robotics engineers fabricated the parts for my force plate prototype from my CAD drafts. See the prototype →

  • Checked incoming parts against specifications
  • Prepared assembly stations and helped assemble components
  • Weekly during the school term, 3 times a week during vacations
