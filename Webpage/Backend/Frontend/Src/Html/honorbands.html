Honor Bands

Selected as first-chair bass clarinet in two honor bands, auditioning against musicians from across Korea and around the world.

KIMEA Honor Band

Bass Clarinet, First Chair · Grades 10–11

The Korean International Music Educators Association (KIMEA) Honor Band brings together student musicians from international schools across Korea.

I auditioned against instrument players from more than 10 international schools in Korea and was selected as first chair for bass clarinet.

  • Auditioned against players from 10+ international schools in Korea
  • Selected as first chair, bass clarinet

AMIS High School Honor Band

Bass Clarinet, First Chair · Grades 10–11

The Association for Music in International Schools (AMIS) High School Honor Band draws student musicians from international schools in many countries.

I auditioned against instrument players from more than 60 international schools in over 10 countries and was selected as first chair for bass clarinet in the high school honor band.

  • Auditioned against players from 60+ international schools in 10+ countries
  • Selected as first chair, bass clarinet
